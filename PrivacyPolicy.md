# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is meant to help you understand what data we collect, why we collect
it, and what we do with it.


<br><br><!-- Section spacer -->


## Information we collect

### Our App

To use our app, we do not require an account or any personal information.

We collect limited, non-personal information from users to ensure proper function.

Like any website, information that is automatically collected in server logs includes:
  * IP addresses
  * App version, app language, the date and time of requests, the query
    parameters of the request and any geolocation data that comes through CloudFlare, our
    caching host.


<br><br><!-- Section spacer -->


## What we don’t use and/or collect

We do not collect or store any personally identifiable information.
If a user creates an account, we will store the email address used 

We do not use third-party cookies or third-party page analytics beyond CloudFlare’s
native analytics as part of caching.
